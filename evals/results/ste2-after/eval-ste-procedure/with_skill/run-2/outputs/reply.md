Assumed context: none. `audience` and `revision_stage` (first draft) come from AGENTS.md. The unit is one pasted methods paragraph (three sentences), with a direct rewrite.

- **Voice:** past-tense passive for procedure, "respondents" for participants, and compact noun phrases such as "analytic sample" and "merged file". I kept all of these.
- **Reader map:** a methodologist who knows screening, coding, and raking in general. They need to see the order this study's steps ran in. "Raking weights" is standard for this audience, so I left it.
- **Main problem:** the order of steps is only implied. Two long sentences chain several steps with commas.

1. **Sentence 1:** it carries screening, recoding, and exclusion. The exclusion rule hangs on as a trailing "with" clause.
2. **Sentence 2:** it packs four things: who coded, how disagreements were resolved, the merge, and the merge key. Coding and merging are two steps, and they appear in order only because of the commas.
3. **Orderings the text fixes:** the coded categories exist before they are merged, the weights go on the merged file, and weighting comes before estimation. The run-on sentences hide these.
4. **N = 2,184:** it closes the paragraph after the weighting sentence, so it reads as the weighted sample. Your own words, "after exclusions", tie it to the exclusion rule.
5. **"Would have been expected to have been applied":** this leaves open whether the weights were applied. That is a matter of substance, so I kept the wording and raised it as a question.
6. **"(4.1 minutes)":** it could be the median or the one-third threshold. I left it where it was and ask below.
7. **Replicators:** the paragraph never separates steps a replicator must redo from inputs they can reuse. That is also a question for you.

## 2. Revised text

```
Responses were screened and recoded after the field period closed. Respondents were excluded if they failed both attention checks or finished in under one third of the median completion time (4.1 minutes). After these exclusions, the analytic sample is N = 2,184. Two research assistants coded the open-ended answers using the codebook in Appendix B, and disagreements were resolved by discussion. The coded categories were then merged with the panel's demographic file using the respondent ID. Raking weights would have been expected to have been applied to the merged file before estimation, matching the 2020 Census margins for age, sex, and region.
```
