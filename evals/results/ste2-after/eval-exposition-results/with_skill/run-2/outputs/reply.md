## Diagnosis

The paragraph reads flat because it has no spine. It opens with a callout ("Table 3 reports the estimates") that makes no claim, and every sentence carries the same weight. The finding is that the identity-disclosed coefficient (0.18, then 0.15) holds up under product fixed effects. It is spread across three sentences, and the 1% level appears only in the last one. The one tension, that the interaction clears only the 5% level, is tucked into a final clause. Altitude is fine: the machinery (SEs, levels, columns) is already in the right place.

## Revised text

```
Table 3 reports the estimates: the identity-disclosed indicator has a positive, significant coefficient that survives product fixed effects. The coefficient is 0.18 (SE = 0.04) in column 2; in column 3, after adding product fixed effects, it is 0.15 (SE = 0.05). The interaction with reviewer tenure is 0.09 (SE = 0.03) in column 4. The coefficient on star rating is 0.42 (SE = 0.02) across all specifications. All coefficients are significant at the 1% level except the interaction, which is significant at the 5% level. R-squared rises from 0.31 to 0.47 across the columns.
```

## Change rationale

The paragraph grows from about 85 to about 100 words (+18%). The added words are one claim sentence, built only from your own coefficients and your closing significance sentence. No new substance was added.

- **Opener:** "Table 3 reports the estimates" is kept verbatim. It now continues into the claim, so the reader knows what to look for before the evidence.
- **Sentences 2–3:** The two estimates the claim rests on now sit together in one sentence. The noun phrase is no longer repeated, because the opener already names the indicator.
- **Ending:** The R-squared sentence moves to the end, so the paragraph closes on a whole-specification statistic. The 5% caveat stays next to the estimates it qualifies.
- **Left alone:** Every number, SE, significance level, and the table and column references are unchanged. There are no citations in the passage. No cuts were needed, and I found no safe improvement to the interaction, star-rating and R-squared sentences beyond reordering.
