## Diagnosis

Assumed context: none. The target venue, audience, revision stage (first draft) and core thesis were all supplied.

- **Voice tics:** impersonal, report-style declaratives. The standard sentence shape is "The coefficient on X is N (SE = n)". The paragraph walks through the table column by column and closes with the significance levels.
- **Buried lede:** the identity-disclosed coefficient (0.18, then 0.15 with product fixed effects) is the thesis result. It sits among six equally weighted statements.
- **Flat enumeration:** the paragraph never says how star rating relates to identity disclosure, and that relation is the paper's point.
- **Old before new:** the star-rating coefficient is the fixed background in every column, but it arrives fifth. Putting it first lets the identity coefficients read against it.
- **Separated referent:** "except the interaction" sits two sentences away from the interaction it refers to.
- **Weak ending:** the paragraph ends on a significance footnote.
- **Missing outcome variable:** the paragraph never names the dependent variable, so the reader can't tell what the coefficients measure. I can't fix that from the text on the page.
- **Calibration check:** the interaction (0.09, SE = 0.03) has the same coefficient-to-SE ratio as column 3 (0.15, SE = 0.05), yet it is reported at a different significance level. I left the statement as written.

## Revised text

```
Table 3 reports the estimates. Star rating has a coefficient of 0.42 (SE = 0.02) across all specifications, and identity disclosure carries a coefficient of its own. The coefficient on the identity-disclosed indicator is 0.18 (SE = 0.04) in column 2 and 0.15 (SE = 0.05) in column 3, after adding product fixed effects. The interaction with reviewer tenure is 0.09 (SE = 0.03) in column 4. All coefficients are significant at the 1% level except the interaction, which is significant at the 5% level. R-squared rises from 0.31 to 0.47 across the columns.
```

## Change rationale

Word count rose from about 90 to about 100 (+10%). The growth is one added clause, "and identity disclosure carries a coefficient of its own". It states the paragraph's question in the topic sentence, using only material already in the paragraph.

- **Star rating moved first.** The fixed benchmark now comes before the results that depend on it. The added clause then sets up the question the next sentences answer.
- **Column 2 and column 3 sentences merged.** The two identity estimates now sit side by side, so they read as one result held under a stricter specification. The numbers and the "after adding product fixed effects" qualifier are kept verbatim and still attach to column 3.
- **Significance sentence moved up.** It now follows the interaction sentence directly, so "the interaction" has a visible antecedent.
- **R-squared moved to the end.** The paragraph now closes on a short sentence about fit, not on a significance footnote.
- **Unchanged:** every number, SE, significance level, column reference and the Table 3 callout.
- **Declined:** a sentence interpreting the identity coefficients, for example that identity adds information beyond the rating. It would assert something the paragraph doesn't contain.
